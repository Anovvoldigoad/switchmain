NSC2Switch V2M STATIC AUDIT

BASELINE
- Exact V2K gameplay baseline retained.
- Original main + V2D/P128 30-word runtime delta retained.
- UJ admission/session unchanged.
- StageMove semantics unchanged: specific/default -> HandleStageChange -> Fix actor -> Fix enemy.
- No V2J PostStage gameplay call.

WHY V2L WAS RETIRED BEFORE HARDWARE
- V2L logged fields through pointers captured before HandleStageChange.
- A stage transition can replace/release those objects, so post-transition dereference of old pointers was unnecessary risk.
- V2M re-resolves the complete known stage graph from the two proven globals at every phase.
- It logs pointer identity only; it does not walk arbitrary object fields.

V2M ADDITIONS — DIAGNOSTIC ONLY
1. Fresh stage graph snapshot at pre_specific, post_specific, post_handle, post_fix.
2. FileLoadRequest + FileOpen tracing for custom/stage XFBIN paths.
3. Trace window remains active from cinematic stage entry until stage exit.
4. Native HandleStageChange and PostStage are observed through pass-through trampolines only.
5. Failure paths disarm the broad resource-trace window so it cannot remain accidentally active.

NO NEW GAMEPLAY MUTATION
- no D-pad change
- no voice change
- no P128 change
- no damage override
- no visibility override
- no manual PostStage call
