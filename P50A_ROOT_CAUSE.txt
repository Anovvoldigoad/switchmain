P50A ROOT-CAUSE BASIS
=====================
1. Tobi awakening assets are present in the production CPK; missing mtobawa is rejected.
2. Tobi ships runtime-only conditionprmManager, specialCondParam and ougiAwakeningParam data.
3. A previously audited/tested SC 1.70 PC compatibility fix reports a condition-lookup
   signature mismatch and fixes Tobi D-Pad condition + Kamui continuation generically.
4. Official Switch 1.70 shows the analogous native geometry:
   - main+0x754A80: 32-byte condition descriptor getter with vanilla bound.
   - main+0x777758 / +0x777920: name/hash resolvers bounded at 512.
   - main+0x747870: raw 176-byte condition accessor bounded at 512.
5. Current merged Tobi conditionprm has 512 vanilla + 5 custom = 517 records.
6. Historical P41A hardware still produced SW_MTOB_XH resolved=0 despite installing
   a split high-bank table. P50A therefore extends the active consumer/getter directly.
