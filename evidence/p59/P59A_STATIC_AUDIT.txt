SCOPE

ARCHITECTURE PROVEN IN PAIRED MAIN
Canonical virtual thunk at 0x7B4680:
  F9400008  ldr x8,[x0]
  F9472102  ldr x2,[x8,#0xE40]
  D61F0040  br  x2
Therefore vtable+0xE40 is the action-mode virtual slot.

Base implementation at 0x7B468C:
  begins with a 0x90-byte stack frame and compares w1 against 8;
  w1 is the mode selector (0..8).
The P58 wrong-jutsu PlayAction callsite 0x7B4B2C is inside this base implementation.

Mode-0 evidence in the same function:
  0x7B4758 mov w1,#445
  0x7B4760 ldr x8,[x8,#0x1488]
  0x7B4768 blr x8
  0x7B4AB8 mov w21,w0 (normal e94 path)
  0x7B4B20 mov w1,w21
  0x7B4B2C bl PlayAction 0x766B8C
This is why P59 traces the base entry rather than rewriting action445.
