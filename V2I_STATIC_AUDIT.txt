IMPORTANT 0x766320 CORRECTION
V2G proved that 0x766320 with index930 drives the PL_ANM event stream, including
SW_MTOB_XH and opcode17 Right=100, while also reproducing the visual disappearance.

prove 0x766320 is the wrong low-level animation primitive. What is proven is that
using the full PL_ANM930 path is not yet behaviorally safe on Switch. V2I therefore
keeps it suppressed and tests only the source-proven frame-13 activation core.
